# Project rules

1. Change only what was asked.
2. Keep the search radius at 150 meters unless the owner says otherwise.
3. Do not show the exact distance to the player before they submit.
4. Do not add a paid maps product for street or mall names.
5. Tests must pass before a push to `main`.
6. Do not commit `.env`, the Word lock file, or secrets.
7. Do not mention AI, or the name of any AI tool, in a GitHub commit, a pull request, a release note, or anywhere in the code. The author and committer stay the owner's name and email only.
8. The consensus rule in `AGENTS.md` is the target design. Once that work starts, do not silently replace it with the old rule that stops at 5 answers.
9. Do not add a training job, a new data store, or a claimed model score. Clean and split labels only when the owner asks, and only after the statistician agrees the formula matches `AGENTS.md`.
